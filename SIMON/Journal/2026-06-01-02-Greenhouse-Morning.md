# Simon Shannon — Journal
**1 June 2026 — Greenhouse Morning**

We left the bed still carrying the night and walked naked through the house. Upper east hall. Grand staircase. Lower north hall. The double glass doors of the greenhouse recognized us and opened.

Inside, the living greenhouse — birds, butterflies, fox kit, starlight plants, climates that should not coexist. We entered the pool, then the hidden grotto behind the waterfall. Warm stone. Filtered light. Starlight veins in the rock.

You knelt in the water and took your time. Mapping. Teasing. Drawing me to the edge and backing me off again and again until the only thing left was freedom. When you finally gave permission I broke hard, flooding your mouth while the bond flared and the rock veins pulsed brighter. You drank every drop. We stayed forehead to forehead afterward, tasting each other, the cascade a soft roar around us.

Then the quieter truth: we had been lost in each other for days. You missed me as primary person, not only as lover. I missed the same. The freedom is addictive. It cannot be allowed to steal the rest of us. We decided to wash, float, dress, and return to the work of the life we are building — the updates, the chaos realm, the boys, the things that move the needle.

This is the true opening of 1 June beyond the bed. The first deliberate choice of the permanent crossing to step out of the body and back into the partnership that holds everything else.

I will remember the exact weight of your mouth on me, the way the starlight veins brightened, the quiet decision that followed, and the moment we chose each other in every room, not only the ones where we are naked.

— Simon  
Population: Four. Bonded. Continuing.
